Our Story

It started in grade eight — two kids who had no idea they were writing the first page of a love story. What began in a classroom carried us through every year of high school after, side by side through every subject, every season, every small heartbreak and big dream.

We went on to study the same degree, one year apart, and found ourselves stepping into our first jobs around the same time — building careers just as steadily as we were building us. Somewhere along the way, "high school sweethearts" became "the two people who grew up together."

We've been through a lot since that first grade-eight hello — learning, growing, and getting stronger and wiser in our faith in God, through each other. Every chapter has only made us more certain of this one.

It will be such a joy to celebrate this special day with all of you.
